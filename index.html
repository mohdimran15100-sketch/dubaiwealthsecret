How to use it in five simple steps

No technique to master. The whole point is that it’s effortless — press play and let the audio do the work.

1

Set aside five quiet minutes

Pick a moment you won’t be interrupted — first thing in the morning, on a lunch break, or before bed. The exact time matters less than doing it regularly.

2

Put on headphones

The track is built from layered sound frequencies, and headphones deliver the full effect. Nothing fancy needed — everyday earbuds work perfectly well.

3

Press play and relax

That’s it. No meditating, no affirmations, no forcing positive thoughts. Sit comfortably and let the five-minute session play in the background.

4

Stay consistent for 21–30 days

Like any habit, the value builds with repetition. Listening daily for a few weeks — without skipping too often — gives your mind time to settle into a calmer, more focused baseline.

Take real-world action

The audio supports your mindset, but the results come from what you do next. Use the clearer, calmer headspace to make more considered decisions, steadier spending choices, and to notice opportunities you might otherwise rush past.
